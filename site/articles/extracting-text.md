# Extracting text

This guide is being written for v1 ([#270](https://github.com/michaelstonis/Broadside/issues/270)). Like the [reading guide](reading.md), its code will come from compiled samples that CI runs.

It will show, with runnable samples: extracting a page's text with its positions, reading order, and the layout tree of a page (blocks, lines, tables, figures).

Until then, `PdfPage.ExtractText()` and `PdfPage.ExtractLayout()` are documented in the [API reference](../api/readme.md).
