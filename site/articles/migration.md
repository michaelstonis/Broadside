# Migration

Guides for moving from PdfSharp, iText and PdfPig are planned after v1 ([#271](https://github.com/michaelstonis/Broadside/issues/271)). Each will map the common tasks of that library to Broadside, side by side, and call out the differences in behavior: lenient reading with diagnostics, and a document model that is a live view over the file's objects rather than a copy.

Until then, [Getting started](getting-started.md) and the [reading guide](reading.md) show the equivalents of the most common tasks: opening, inspecting, changing and saving a file.
