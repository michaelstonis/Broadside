# Hand-labelled figures and captions of real-world untagged pages, for RealWorldFigureDetectionTests (issue #190).
# Each "== <corpus path> <page number>" section lists every figure drawn with graphics on the page (code listings set in text
# only are not figures to geometry and are left out), and the captions of its tables.
# "F x0 y0 x1 y1 | caption" is a figure: its box in default user space and the start of its caption ("-" when it has none).
# "T | caption" is a table and the start of its caption.
# Boxes come from the page, not from the detector: the bounds of the non-white pixels of a 72 dpi Ghostscript render with text
# filtered out (-dFILTERTEXT), between the text line above the figure and its caption in poppler's -bbox-layout; a rule across
# the column over or under a float, with white space around it, separates it from the text and does not count. Matching is by
# overlap (intersection over union at least 0.5), so a box need not be exact. Captions are matched by their start, compared in
# compatibility form (NFKC); prefixes stop before ligatures the text extraction maps to other characters.
