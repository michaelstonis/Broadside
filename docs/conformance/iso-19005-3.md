# ISO 19005-3:2012 PDF/A-3 (output)

ISO 19005-3 is not purchased (open decision 12): rows are keyed by the clause numbers of veraPDF's PDF/A-3B and PDF/A-3U validation profiles (veraPDF 1.28, `PDFA-3B.xml`, `PDFA-3U.xml`), and the Notes cite their rule identifiers. PDF/A-3 is PDF/A-2 plus embedded files of any kind as associated files: only the clauses whose rules differ from ISO 19005-2 have a row here; every other clause is implemented and tested as its row in [iso-19005-2.md](./iso-19005-2.md) says, for `PdfAConformance.PdfA3b` and `PdfA3u` alike (issue #202).
