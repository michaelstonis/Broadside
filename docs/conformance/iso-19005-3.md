# ISO 19005-3:2012 PDF/A-3 (output)

ISO 19005-3 is not purchased (open decision 12): rows are keyed by the clause numbers of veraPDF's PDF/A-3B and PDF/A-3U validation profiles (veraPDF 1.28, `PDFA-3B.xml`, `PDFA-3U.xml`; level A, #650, `PDFA-3A.xml`), and the Notes cite their rule identifiers.
