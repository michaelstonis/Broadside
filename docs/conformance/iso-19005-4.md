# ISO 19005-4:2020 PDF/A-4 (output)

ISO 19005-4 is not purchased (open decision 12): rows are keyed by the clause numbers of veraPDF's PDF/A-4 and PDF/A-4F validation profiles (veraPDF 1.28, `PDFA-4.xml`, `PDFA-4F.xml`), and the Notes cite their rule identifiers. Titles paraphrase the profiles' rule descriptions. A save with `PdfAConformance.PdfA4` or `PdfA4f` (issue #203) runs the same check step as PDF/A-2 (`PdfAChecker`, see [iso-19005-2.md](./iso-19005-2.md)), reporting each rule under the PDF/A-4 identifier (ISO 19005-4 renumbers 6.1.6 to 6.12), refuses in strict mode or saves without claiming conformance in lenient mode, and writes a PDF 2.0 file. PDF/A-4e is not implemented. Status: `not started` | `partial` | `done` | `n/a`.
