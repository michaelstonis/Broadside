# Sponsoring Broadside

Broadside is MIT licensed and always will be. Sponsorship through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/michaelstonis) pays for the tooling and infrastructure the project runs on (AI tooling, CI, signing) and moves your bugs and feature requests earlier in the queue. It never buys features, and no part of the library is ever reserved for sponsors. The reasoning is in [ADR 0008](docs/adr/0008-mit-license-sponsorship-not-restriction.md).

> **Sponsorship buys *priority*, never *access*. No feature is ever gated behind a tier.**

The monthly goal is **$200**, which covers the project's running costs.

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | $5 a month | Your name in this file. Priority level 4. |
| **Individual, your amount** | Any amount from $6 a month | Your name in this file. Priority level 3. |
| **Company** | $150 a month | Your logo in the README and on the documentation site. Priority level 2. |
| **Enterprise** | Engagements from $5,000, through [TheEightBot](https://eightbot.com) | Scoped work, planned and invoiced per engagement. With your permission, a large logo as an Enterprise partner. Priority level 1. |

That is the whole list of promises: recognition, and a place in the queue.

- **Priority is order, not a deadline.** Issues are triaged highest level first; no tier carries a response time, a fix-by date, a vote, a call or a support channel.
- **Priority needs an active monthly sponsorship.** One-time sponsorships are welcome and get your name listed here, but no priority level.
- **Issues are labeled automatically.** Issues opened by an active sponsor get a `sponsor:company` or `sponsor:individual` label, so the order is visible to everyone.

Every tier receives the same library with the same features, under the same license, on the same release schedule. A sponsor can move a bug or a feature earlier in the queue; a sponsor cannot have a bug fixed or a feature built that is withheld from anyone else.

## Enterprise

Need something built on a schedule, help integrating Broadside, or an invoice your procurement team can process? Contact [TheEightBot](https://eightbot.com). Engagements are scoped and quoted individually and start at $5,000. Anything beyond the tiers above, including annual or invoiced sponsorships, goes through this route.

Work on Broadside itself done under an engagement lands in this repository under the MIT license, available to everyone. Code specific to your application stays yours.
