# Sponsoring Broadside

Broadside is MIT licensed and always will be. Sponsorship through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/michaelstonis) funds the work and buys priority on bugs and features. It never buys features, and no part of the library is ever reserved for sponsors. The reasoning is in [ADR 0008](docs/adr/0008-mit-license-sponsorship-not-restriction.md).

> **Sponsorship buys *priority*, never *access*. No feature is ever gated behind a tier.**

## Tiers

| Tier | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Individual** | Our thanks, and your name listed in this file. |
| **Company** | Priority bug triage, and a monthly vote on what gets built next. |
| **Enterprise** | A direct support channel, feature requests scheduled into the plan, and your logo in the README. |

Every tier receives the same library with the same features, under the same license, on the same release schedule. A sponsor can move a bug or a feature earlier in the queue; a sponsor cannot have a bug fixed or a feature built that is withheld from anyone else.
