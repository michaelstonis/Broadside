3. Font encodings (ISO 32000-2:2020 Annex D)
---------------------------------------------

StandardEncoding, WinAnsiEncoding, MacRomanEncoding, MacExpertEncoding and the Mac OS Roman additions of Table 113
(src/Broadside/Fonts/Data/encodings.txt) are the code-to-glyph-name assignments of ISO 32000-2:2020 Annex D and
§9.6.5.4, cross-checked against the AFM files above and pdf.js (Apache-2.0); no pdf.js code or data is included.
ISOLatin1Encoding, which a Type 1 program may name as its built-in encoding, is the code-to-glyph-name assignment of the
PostScript Language Reference, third edition, Appendix E.7 (with the names of Appendix E.5).


4. The Independent JPEG Group's software (via libjpeg-turbo), in the DCTDecode filter
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This software is based in part on the work of the Independent JPEG Group.

Source:   https://github.com/libjpeg-turbo/libjpeg-turbo, commit 43ea809765004508d8da72ebe562cf570c6a62b2:
          src/jidctint.c (jpeg_idct_islow, 8-bit and 12-bit), src/jdcolor.c (build_ycc_rgb_table, ycc_rgb_convert,
          ycck_cmyk_convert), src/jdarith.c (arith_decode) and src/jaricom.c (jpeg_aritab)
Licence:  the IJG License in README.ijg (LEGAL ISSUES section), shipped in this package as licenses/ijg/README.ijg and in
          the repository as src/Broadside/Filters/Dct/README.ijg (SHA-256
          75815e3bf6484201a3c3d17a1bbf10f2e8e3237f84df10a2357ea896db2a81d6). libjpeg-turbo's LICENSE.md places code
          inherited from libjpeg, and modifications to it, under that licence.
Used as:  src/Broadside/Filters/Dct/InverseDct.cs (the integer inverse DCT and its range-limit tables),
          src/Broadside/Filters/Dct/JpegColor.cs (the fixed-point YCbCr and YCCK conversions) and
          src/Broadside/Filters/Dct/ArithmeticDecoder.cs (the QM decoder's register layout and the packed form of the
          ITU-T T.81 Table D.3 state machine, with a fixed-probability state).
Modified: yes. Translated to C#; coefficients in natural order with an int quantization table, 32-bit arithmetic for 8-bit
          samples and 64-bit for 12-bit samples, the YCC tables built as int arrays indexed by sample (computed inline for
          12-bit samples), YCCK computed from the same tables. The rest of the decoder (marker parsing, Huffman and
          arithmetic statistical models, progressive scan decoding, scan and restart control, upsampling) is written from
          ITU-T T.81, checked against src/jdphuff.c and src/jdarith.c; its handling of out-of-sequence restart markers
          follows the decision table of jpeg_resync_to_restart (src/jdmarker.c).
